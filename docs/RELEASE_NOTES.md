**Install:** download `NumDash.nwa` below, connect your calculator by USB, open [my.numworks.com/apps](https://my.numworks.com/apps) and upload the file. Then open **NumDash** from the home screen.

A near-complete rebuild of NumDash as a faithful Geometry Dash recreation:

- The seven first official levels, Stereo Madness to Jumper, with their original layouts, colour changes and secret coins.
- Geometry Dash 2.x cube and ship physics at 240 Hz; every level is verified completable by a recorded replay.
- Redrawn in the style of the game: textured background and ground, glowing blocks, pulsing orbs, portals, particles, trails, the attempt counter and progress bar.
- Original screens: main menu with the running icon, level select, pause menu, practice mode with checkpoints, "New Best!", the full level-complete sequence (light rays, fireworks, the window on chains), icon kit colours, settings, stats and a level editor with three save slots.
- Safe saves: records are never moved behind Epsilon's back, and progress from the previous NumDash version is kept.

Controls: OK, EXE or Up to jump, Back to pause, 0 and Backspace for practice checkpoints, Home to save and quit.
