**How to install:** download `NumDash.nwa` below, plug your NumWorks calculator into your computer, open [my.numworks.com/apps](https://my.numworks.com/apps) and send the file to the calculator. Then open **NumDash** from the Home screen.

**What's new:** NumDash is now a much closer copy of Geometry Dash.

- The first seven real levels, from Stereo Madness to Jumper, with their hidden coins.
- Jumping, gravity and the ship feel like the original game.
- New graphics, effects and menus that look like Geometry Dash: the main menu, level select, pause menu, "New Best!", practice mode and the full "Level Complete!" celebration.
- Pick your icon colours, change settings and build your own levels.
- Your old progress is kept.

**Controls:** OK, EXE or Up to jump, Back to pause, 0 to place a checkpoint in practice mode, Home to save and quit.
